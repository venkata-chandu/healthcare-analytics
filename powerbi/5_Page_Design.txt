POWER BI 5-PAGE BUILD SPEC

PAGE 1 — Executive Overview
Cards: Total Patients, Critical Cases, Admissions, Avg Wait, Bed Occupancy, Avg LOS
Visuals: patient arrivals by date; emergency vs admission; department workload; triage mix; bed occupancy trend.
Slicers: Date, Department, Triage.

PAGE 2 — Emergency Department Analytics
Visuals: arrivals by hour; arrivals by day of week; triage distribution; average waiting time by triage; arrival mode; condition ranking.
Goal: identify demand peaks and emergency bottlenecks.

PAGE 3 — Patient & Disease Analytics
Visuals: age bands; gender; condition ranking; condition vs admission rate; region; insurance mix.
Goal: understand who arrives and why.

PAGE 4 — Hospital Operations & Resources
Visuals: bed occupancy by department; occupied/available beds; average LOS by department; average wait by department; doctor workload.
Goal: identify operational bottlenecks.

PAGE 5 — Forecasting & Decision Support
Use a line chart for daily emergency arrivals with forecast where appropriate.
Add alert cards:
- Bed occupancy > 90%
- Avg waiting time > 30 min
- Critical cases > threshold
Add a table of departments with current occupancy, average wait, and demand.
